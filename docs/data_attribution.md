# Data credit and reuse boundaries

This study analyses the *Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist*, published by Olist on Kaggle and listed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International. The analysis links selected sources, filters to eligible order ratings and computes new aggregate estimates and diagnostics. [Dataset listing](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce) · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) · [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/)

The study joins selected order, item, seller, and review records in DuckDB. It keeps eligible reviews from unambiguous single-seller orders, splits scores by answer timestamp, and computes review-volume-adjusted estimates, evaluation errors, and diagnostics. The public figures and report captures present cohort and review-band summaries calculated from those steps. See the [methodology](methodology.md) for the eligibility and evaluation rules.

| Material | Release boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Original research commentary and figures | Released with independently calculated aggregate statistical findings and Olist attribution. |
| Power BI Overview and Validation images | Released as static captures of original report pages showing aggregate findings, study settings, and hypothetical examples. |
| Synthetic examples and fixtures | Released as original hypothetical material. The calculator uses the study’s pooled reference and strength. |
| Original project software | All rights reserved. |
| Olist records, processed databases, seller and order outputs, and the populated Power BI report | Retained locally. Obtain the source archive from the dataset listing under its applicable terms. |

The public release is limited to the original materials and aggregate findings listed above. Olist’s CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms apply to its source material and any covered adaptations. Assess proposed reuse of that material under the applicable terms. [CC license legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.en)
